Dynamic Filter and ADSR Envelope Simulator

Welcome! Follow these steps to explore how filters and envelopes sculpt electronic audio over time:

  • Explore Filter Types: Select between Low-Pass, High-Pass, and Band-Pass modes. Drag the Cutoff Frequency to hear how it shapes a bright Sawtooth wave.
  • Test Resonance Peak: Increase the Resonance (Q) slider to add a whistle-like quality directly at the cutoff point.
  • Shape the Volume (ADSR): Set Attack to 500ms, Decay to 300ms, Sustain to 40%, and Release to 1000ms. Hold the virtual keyboard to see the curve tracer move.
  • Apply LFO Modulation: Select "Filter Cutoff" as the LFO target, set Rate to 4Hz, and listen to the rhythmic "wah-wah" sweep.
